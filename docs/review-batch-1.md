# Review batch 1: the first 30 prompts

Status: **waiting for Stijn's review** (written 14 November 2026). These set the voice for the other 336, so say what to change: too long, too dry, too leading, wrong themes, anything.

The rules they follow: one or two sentences; a question that makes the writer decide something about their own nation; nothing about a real country; realistic geofiction (no magic, aliens or fantasy races, technology up to today); British English; 200 characters at most, so a prompt fits a forum title and the card.

The voice is taken from the five prompts on the mock-up: a plain fact about "your nation", then a pointed question, often about who gains, who pays or who resents it.

## Politics

1. Your parliament has a rule everyone agrees is absurd and nobody will repeal. What is it, and who does it quietly protect?
2. One ministry in your government is where careers go to die. Which one, and what did the last minister do to be sent there?
3. A region of your nation votes against the government every single time. What does it want that it never gets?
4. Your head of state has one power they have never dared to use. What would have to happen first?

## History

5. Every schoolchild in your nation learns one date by heart. What happened then, and which part do the textbooks leave out?
6. There is a statue in your capital that half the country would like taken down. Who is it of, and why is it still standing?
7. Your nation once lost a war it still calls a draw. What did the peace really cost?
8. An old family name still opens doors in your nation. What did that family do, and how long ago?

## Culture

9. Visitors to your nation always commit the same breach of manners. What is it, and how do the locals let them know?
10. One song is played at every wedding in your nation. What is it about, and why do the grandparents wince?
11. Your national dish started as poor people's food. What is in it, and who serves the expensive version now?
12. A sport nobody else takes seriously fills stadiums in your nation. Which rule does a foreigner never understand?

## Economy

13. One company employs so many people that your government asks it before deciding anything. What does it make, and what does it get away with?
14. Your coins carry a face or a symbol that people argue about. What is it, and who wants it replaced?
15. There is something your nation has to import and hates importing. What is it, and who sells it?
16. A town in your nation was built around one industry, and the industry has just closed. What happens to the town?

## Military

17. Your armed forces keep one tradition that makes no military sense at all. What is it, and what happened the last time someone tried to end it?
18. Does your nation have conscription? Say who serves, who gets out of it, and how.
19. Your army's most decorated regiment is recruited from a single region. Why there, and what does the region expect in return?
20. Your nation keeps a military base far from home. What deal put it there, and who thinks it was a bad one?

## Geography

21. One river in your nation floods every few years, and people keep living beside it. What keeps them there?
22. Your second city insists it should be the first. What does it have that the capital doesn't?
23. There is a corner of your nation that every map shows and almost nobody visits. What is out there, and who lives there anyway?
24. A mountain pass, a strait or a bridge: pick the one place your nation cannot afford to lose. What happens on the day it closes?

## Daily life

25. It is seven in the morning in your nation. What is breakfast, how do people get to work, and what is everyone complaining about?
26. What do people in your nation queue for without complaint, and what would start a riot if they had to queue for it?
27. A child in your nation turns sixteen. What are they suddenly allowed to do, and what is still three years away?

## Characters

28. Your nation's most trusted public figure is not a politician. Who are they, and what would it take to ruin them?
29. A civil servant has held the same desk for forty years and outlasted eleven ministers. What do they know?
30. Name the journalist your government fears most. What story are they sitting on?
